About AI Translator Pro

AI Translator Pro is a free, lightweight, and high-speed web application designed to translate long articles, academic documents, news stories, and everyday messages across major international languages.

Our tool requires no user registration, no login, and no account setup. Simply paste your text, select your target language, and get accurate translations in real-time.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is fundamental to us. Here is how we safeguard your information:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not require sign-up, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • No Content Storage: Texts pasted into the translator are processed instantly in your browser session and are never saved on our servers.
  • Client-Side Processing: All translation tasks happen securely using standard encrypted API requests.
